BenefitHR	Fair Benefit Distribution

BenefitHR: A tool for fair benefit distribution, targeted task assignment, roll call, and activity grouping. It supports employee list import, multi-batch benefit allocation, custom allocation rules, big-screen display, and result export for registration. It is an excellent helper for HR and administrative staff to distribute benefits. The allocation process is transparent and fair. All data is stored locally offline without internet access. Verified by tens of thousands of enterprise users over the years, it has now been polished and upgraded to the Pro version.

*Target Users & Scenarios
‑ Available for individual users and all types of enterprises
‑ Suitable for benefit distribution, targeted task assignment, roll call, and activity grouping. It meets various scenarios such as fair and targeted allocation of limited benefits and tasks to specific personnel.

*Basic Distribution
Carry out batch benefit issuance. You can customize benefit names and the number of people per batch, and flexibly configure allocation rules to adapt to various benefit distribution scenarios.
‑ List Import: Supports importing single-column lists from txt and CSV files, as well as manual addition of personnel. Batch entry is available via pasting by line or comma separation. Employees with identical names can be distinguished by department information.
‑ Allocation Modes: Two optional modes: animated batch distribution display and one-click quick distribution (skip animation).
‑ Distribution Rules: You can set whether selected personnel can participate in subsequent batches again, or exclude personnel selected in previous batches for more flexible configuration of the final batch.
‑ Result Records: Each round of allocation records is saved automatically. You can search and view historical distribution details.
‑ Export Receipt Records: Supports exporting CSV receipt registration forms, or directly copying table content to paste into Excel for offline printing and registration.

*Big-Screen Display
Customizable activity display page, suitable for screen projection at benefit issuance events to enhance the sense of ceremony.
‑ Theme Colors: Built-in 7 festive themes: Festive Red, Warm Sun Orange, Peach Blossom Pink, Gilded, Green Forest, Navy Blue, Taro Purple. Switch with one click and take effect immediately.
‑ Music Settings: Built-in background music and prompt sound effects. Local audio files can be uploaded, and sound effects can be toggled freely.
‑ Full-Screen List: Display the list of selected personnel in full screen for public display on on-site large screens.
‑ Brand Information: Customize brand name, benefit title and description, together with promotional images. Suitable for internal team display of benefits and the application.

*Administrative Assistant
Simple and intuitive operation, tailored to the daily work needs of administrative and HR staff.
‑ List Management: Search employees, clear the entire list in batches, add/edit/delete personnel individually to quickly maintain the personnel list.
‑ Result Management: Delete single records or clear all distribution records. After deletion, corresponding personnel will regain allocation eligibility.
‑ Sharing Function: Share distribution results for colleagues to check issuance information collaboratively. Receipt forms can be printed directly for registration.
‑ Configuration Verification: Automatically verify configurations before distribution and remind you of issues such as insufficient list members or abnormal batch parameters to avoid on-site errors.
‑ CSV File Management: One-stop management for exporting, printing, copying, renaming and deleting files.
‑ Offline Operation: All data is stored on the local device. No internet connection required, and data will not be uploaded to the cloud to guarantee data security.

福利HR	公平福利派发

BenefitHR 公平福利派发、任务定向分配、人员点名、活动分组工具，支持员工名单导入、多批次福利分配、自定义分配规则、大屏展示、结果导出登记，是人事行政福利发放好帮手，分配透明公正，全程离线本地存储，无需联网。多年经数万企业用户长期验证，现已打磨升级 Pro 版本。

*面向用户及场景
‑ 个人用户与各类企业均可使用
‑ 适用于福利派发、任务定向分配、人员点名、活动分组，满足福利、任务限额公平定向到人等多种场景

*基础派发
批量开展福利发放，可自定义福利名称、每批分配人数，灵活配置分配规则，适配各类福利发放场景。
‑ 名单导入：支持txt、csv单列名单导入，也支持手动新增人员；支持按行粘贴或逗号分隔批量录入，员工重名可搭配部门信息区分
‑ 分配模式：逐批动画派发展示、一键快捷派发（跳过动画）两种模式可选
‑ 派发规则：可设置允许已入选人员重复参与后续批次，也能指定排除往期批次已入选人员，收尾批次配置更灵活
‑ 结果记录：每一轮分配记录自动保存，支持检索查看历史派发详情
‑ 导出领取：支持导出CSV领取登记表，也可直接复制表格内容粘贴至Excel，方便线下打印登记

*大屏展示
自定义活动展示页面，适合福利发放现场投屏，增强活动仪式感
‑ 主题配色：内置7款喜庆主题，喜庆红、暖阳橙、桃花粉、鎏金、青森、藏青、香芋紫，一键切换即时生效
‑ 音乐设置：自带背景音乐与提示音效，支持上传本地音频，音效可自由开关
‑ 全屏名单：全屏展示入选人员名单，适配现场大屏公示
‑ 品牌信息：自定义品牌名称、福利标题和说明，搭配宣传配图，福利及App适配团队内部使用展示

*行政助手
操作简洁易用，贴合行政人事日常工作需求
‑ 名单管理：支持员工搜索、批量清空名单、单独新增/编辑/删除人员，快速维护人员清单
‑ 结果管理：支持单条删除或清空全部派发记录，删除记录后对应人员恢复可分配资格
‑ 分享功能：可分享派发结果，方便同事协同核对发放信息，领取表单支持直接打印登记
‑ 配置校验：派发前自动核验配置，提示名单不足、批次参数异常等问题，规避现场出错
‑ CSV文件管理：导出、打印、复制、重命名、删除文件一站式管理
‑ 离线运行：全部数据保存在本地设备，无需联网，信息不上传云端，保障数据安全
